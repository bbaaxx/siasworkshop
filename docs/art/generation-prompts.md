# Art generation prompts

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-09. The two texture concepts
were refined into native 16×16 pixel art with `tools/gen_final_textures.py`.
The storefront logo is the generated image, saved separately from mod resources.
Vanilla compass and cherry textures were inspected locally for reference;
no vanilla pixels were copied into the artwork.

## Wilderness Compass concept

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Minecraft 16x16 pixel-art item texture design, shown greatly enlarged with crisp square pixels. Generate ONE original Wilderness Compass with transparent background. Familiar vanilla Minecraft small round/octagonal pocket compass silhouette, front-on almost flat, stout rim, no hanging loop. Unique sakura/cherry blossom identity: pale blush ivory dial, cherry wood dark plum-brown outer edging, rose-pink inner rim, tiny understated blossom petal motifs embedded in the dial, high-contrast deep raspberry directional needle pointing straight up, muted plum counterweight pointing down, small pale center pivot. Limited palette, chunky 1-pixel steps on a strict virtual 16x16 grid; all details must work at native 16x16 resolution. Center pivot at (8,8), entire item fits in the square with transparent corners. Vanilla Minecraft cozy understated pixel art, not polished vector illustration. No words, letters, numbers, shadow outside sprite, antialiasing, scene, mockup, gradients or extra items. Make the needle legible above the pale floral dial. This is concept artwork to refine into a production sprite.

## Village Foundation concept

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single Minecraft 16x16 opaque block texture shown enlarged in crisp pixel art. Village Foundation ceremonial cornerstone, cozy vanilla Minecraft cherry blossom theme. Straight orthographic flat square texture filling the entire image edge to edge, NOT a 3D block. Warm grey stone slab perimeter with subtle masonry joints and a narrow beveled inner border. Inset center of dusty pink cherry planks, horizontal grain, a small restrained pale pink sakura blossom inlay at the exact center. Understated building material that belongs beside vanilla stone bricks and cherry planks. Strong simple clusters and strict virtual 16x16 grid, limited palette, one-pixel steps, no gradients, no fine detail smaller than one virtual pixel. Fully opaque, square, no margins, no background surrounding the texture, no text, no labels, no watermark. Must read as a foundation cornerstone rather than a chest, ore, or utility machine. This is concept artwork to refine into an exact production texture.

## CurseForge logo

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: square 1024x1024 PNG project icon for a Minecraft mod called Sia's Workshop. No text or lettering. Bold original pixel-art emblem readable at tiny storefront list size. A large round sakura-themed compass occupies almost the whole frame: chunky dark plum cherry-wood rim, rose-pink highlights, pale ivory/blush face, small pink blossom-petal markings, strong raspberry needle pointing up and a muted plum counterweight. A short flowering cherry twig with two clear five-petaled sakura blossoms overlaps the upper-right rim; a small warm-grey cut-stone foundation plinth beneath the compass anchors it. Keep the compass dominant and the blossoms simple. Cozy vanilla-inspired game pixel art, crisp square pixel steps on a virtual 64x64 grid, restrained palette, flat color clusters, no smooth gradients or antialiasing. Centered balanced composition, large recognizable silhouette, moderate padding, solid deep charcoal-plum background (#292630), no frame around the image, no other objects, no watermark, no photorealism. Match the pale pink, raspberry, warm stone and plum material palette of the mod's in-game textures. This is the final storefront icon, not a sprite sheet or a mockup.
