## Delivered assets

Final 16x16 Village Foundation and all 32 Wilderness Compass PNGs live at the original siasworkshop texture paths. The foundation is opaque; compass alpha is binary with an identical floral dial and silhouette in every frame. A square storefront logo is at docs/art/logo.png, outside mod resources.

## Reproduction and references

Run python3 tools/gen_final_textures.py with Pillow to regenerate the final pixel artwork and static review sheets. Never overwrite final art with the historical gen_stub_textures.py. Original built-in ImageGen concepts, exact prompts and delivery details are under docs/art/. Vanilla assets were visual references only; no vanilla pixels were copied.

## Compass resource wiring

The hand-written angle table originally referenced 31 missing frame models. ModItemModelProvider now generates 32 frame-model JSONs. Run ./gradlew runData, then ./gradlew build separately: Gradle can package resources before runData finishes in a combined invocation. The packaged JAR was checked for every override model, texture resolution and source-identical PNG bytes.

## Verification limits

All sprites pass dimensions/alpha checks, the 32 directions are unique, and non-needle pixels remain invariant. Needle centroids are within 10 degrees of their intended directions at 16x16. Datagen and build passed; static enlarged previews were inspected. An interactive in-game check remains a useful visual follow-up. This work covers the current 1.21.1 checkout, not a release or port to the 1.20.1 branch.
